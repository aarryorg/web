AI Search Strategy: The Seen & Trusted Brand Framework (S&T GEO)

AI is already reshaping how buyers discover and choose brands. We propose the Seen & Trusted (S&T) Framework to help your brand systematically earn both mentions and citations.

By Aarry Strategy Team • Sep. 26, 2025 • 15 min read

When someone asks an LLM (like ChatGPT or Google AI Mode) about your category, two things happen: your brand is mentioned in the answer, or your content is cited as proof. Most companies achieve one, but very few achieve both. The gap is the opportunity.

The S&T Framework helps you multiply visibility, trust, and conversions by aligning your efforts across platforms. SEO remains the foundation, but AI pulls signals from review sites, Reddit, news coverage, and support docs. When these signals are fragmented, your competitors win the conversation.

Analyze your current AI visibility.

Why AI Search Strategy Isn’t Just SEO’s Job

Your content team can optimize every page on your site and still lose AI visibility to a competitor with weaker rankings but stronger brand signals. AI systems pull signals from everywhere, not just your website, mining the web for nuanced, third-party context.

The Distributed Signal Challenge

AI visibility is a product of synchronized, cross-departmental efforts. If one signal is weak, the entire brand sentiment suffers. Below are the key teams that drive the non-SEO signals AI relies on:

Customer Success & Reviews

Drives high-quality, substantive reviews on G2 and Capterra. AI needs detailed context to cite when comparing products—not just ratings.

Product Team & Transparency

Controls whether pricing, features, and roadmaps are easily findable by the public. Hiding details leads AI to cite outdated or negative speculation from forums.

PR Team & Authority Signals

Lands media coverage and analyst reports. These third-party mentions build the high-level trust signals AI systems use to determine a brand's authority.

Playbook 1 – How to Get Seen (The Sentiment Battle)

Getting "Seen" means earning favorable mentions in AI responses without needing a direct citation link. This is the sentiment battle, where AI characterizes your brand (e.g., "affordable but limited" vs. "powerful and scalable").

The Four Drivers of Favorable Mentions

You must actively shape the context surrounding your brand. Here’s where AI platforms are mining for product characteristics:

Build Presence on the Right Review Sites

Focus on platforms like G2, Capterra, and Amazon. Reward customers for writing reviews that are detailed and substantive—mentioning specific features, use cases, and outcomes, not just high ratings.

Participate in Community Discussions

Be authentically present on Reddit, Quora, and Stack Overflow. Answer questions, address misconceptions, and share knowledge. AI detects and prioritizes helpful, human responses over pure marketing pitches.

Engineer UGC and Social Proof

Encourage users to share their wins and creative use cases publicly (e.g., on LinkedIn). This real-world advocacy becomes visible training data that AI recognizes and amplifies, driving favorable sentiment.

Secure "Best of" List Inclusions

Comparison articles (Forbes, TechRadar) are key sources. Make journalists' jobs easy by providing a dedicated press kit with specs, images, and pricing. Target category expansion to multiply mentions.

Playbook 2 – How to Be Trusted (The Authority Game)

The "Trusted" playbook is about building the technical and factual authority necessary to be cited as evidence by AI systems. This is the authority game, where you prove you are the definitive, reliable source of information.

Five Ways to Build Citation Core Status

AI systems trust sources with verified information, structured data, and established credibility. You need to maximize confidence in what they are citing.

Optimize Your Official Site for AI

Use semantic HTML (tables, lists, clean headings) and Schema Markup to ensure crawlers can easily parse key details. Fast, stable sites (passing Core Web Vitals) are preferred sources.

Maintain Wikipedia & Knowledge Graph Accuracy

Audit your Wikipedia and Google Knowledge Panel quarterly. Fix factual errors using credible third-party citations. This ensures AI's foundational understanding of your brand is correct.

Publish Transparent Pricing

Hidden pricing leads AI to cite negative speculation from forums. Publish clear tier breakdowns, feature comparisons, and limitations to provide reliable, quotable data points.

Expand Documentation & FAQs

AI systems seek detailed, problem-solving content. Create dedicated pages for common problems ("How to integrate X with Y") and use HowTo/FAQ Schema extensively.

Create Original Research That AI Wants to Cite

Publish unique threat reports, industry surveys, or benchmark studies based on proprietary data. Verified, quotable data becomes evidence AI systems need to support their claims.

Pulling It All Together – Running Both Playbooks

The Seen (Sentiment) and Trusted (Authority) playbooks must run simultaneously. You can't afford to wait. The brands winning AI search are testing, learning, and adjusting faster than their competition.

Three Steps to Execution: The S&T Launch Plan

1. Audit

Current AI Visibility

Run test queries in ChatGPT and Google AI Mode for your brand, category, and problems. Note where you are mentioned (Seen) and where you are cited (Trusted). Use this data to identify competitive gaps.

2. Build

Parallel Campaigns

Establish a shared, cross-departmental dashboard. While Customer Success runs review campaigns (Playbook 1), SEO/Engineering must improve site structure and docs (Playbook 2). Coordination is the key.

3. Monitor

Iterate & Adapt

AI visibility shifts fast. Track mentions and citations monthly. If you have strong mentions but weak citations, ramp up Authority (Trusted). If you are cited but rarely mentioned, ramp up Sentiment (Seen).

The AI Visibility Window is Open. Right now, AI visibility is anyone’s game. Move faster on the Seen & Trusted Framework than established giants, and you can capture tomorrow’s buyers.

Frequently Asked Questions about the S&T Framework

The goal is to move beyond simply ranking in organic search results and systematically earn both favorable brand mentions (Seen) and content citations (Trusted) within AI-generated summaries and conversations, such as Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT.

AI systems pull signals from non-SEO sources, including customer reviews (Customer Success), feature transparency (Product Team), media coverage (PR Team), and community discussions (Support Team). Success requires synchronized, cross-departmental coordination.
