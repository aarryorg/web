1. Introduction and Scope

Aarry Limited ("Aarry," "we," "us," or "our") is a Shopify agency specializing in custom Shopify app development, store setup, theme customization, and ongoing store management services (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy describes how Aarry Limited collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our services, interact with our agency website, or use any custom applications we develop for you or our clients.

2. Information We Collect

A. Client Information (Directly from you, our Agency Client)

When you engage Aarry Limited for Services, we collect Personal Information necessary to fulfill our contract, which may include:

  • **Contact Data:** Name, email address, phone number, and business address.
  • **Payment Data:** Billing information and payment details related to our services.
  • **Account Access Data:** Necessary credentials (e.g., staff accounts, API keys) required to access and manage your Shopify store or other third-party services on your behalf. We treat this data with the highest security protocols.
  • **Project Data:** Specifications, documentation, and communications related to your project.

B. App End-User Data (Collected via Apps We Develop)

When we develop a custom Shopify application, that app may collect information from the end customers of the Shopify merchant (our client). Please note the following critical distinction:

  • **Aarry's Role:** In almost all cases, Aarry Limited is a **Data Processor** acting strictly on behalf of the Shopify merchant (our Client), who is the **Data Controller**.
  • **Data Types:** This data may include customer names, email addresses, order history, and browsing behavior within the client's store, depending on the app's function (e.g., loyalty programs, product reviews).
  • **Governing Policy:** The collection and use of this End-User data is primarily governed by the **Privacy Policy of our Client (the Shopify Merchant)**. Aarry Limited only accesses this data as necessary to provide, maintain, and support the application services for the client.

C. Website and Tracking Data

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies (like Google Analytics) to track activity on our own agency website and hold certain information. This includes IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on pages.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To **Provide and Manage Services:** To perform our contractual obligations, including developing and maintaining Shopify apps and managing your store.
  • To **Communicate:** To send you service updates, invoices, technical notices, and support messages.
  • To **Improve Services:** To monitor the performance of our apps and services and make improvements.
  • To **Comply with Law:** To meet our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your Personal Information to marketers or third parties. We may disclose information only in the following circumstances:

  • **With Service Providers:** To third parties who perform services on our behalf (e.g., hosting providers, payment processors, and project management tools), strictly for the purpose of carrying out their work for us.
  • **With Shopify:** As necessary to integrate, submit, or manage applications and stores within the Shopify platform.
  • **Legal Compliance:** When required by law, subpoena, or if we reasonably believe that such action is necessary to comply with the law or the reasonable requests of law enforcement.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect the security of any Personal Information we process. However, no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so we cannot promise or guarantee that hackers, cybercriminals, or other unauthorized third parties will not be able to defeat our security and improperly collect, access, steal, or modify your information.

6. Third-Party Platforms (Facebook, Google, etc.)

When you use this Privacy Policy URL to comply with the requirements of platforms like Facebook, Google, or others for application development, you acknowledge that our data practices may be subject to their respective terms and policies. The data handling specific to any app integration (e.g., using Facebook Login or Google APIs) will be detailed in the app's dedicated listing or documentation.

7. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under data protection laws (such as GDPR or CCPA) regarding your personal information. These may include the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your data. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
