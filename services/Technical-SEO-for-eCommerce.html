Supercharge Your Site Performance

For eCommerce, speed isn't just a ranking factor—it's a revenue driver. A faster site leads to better engagement and higher conversion rates.

Optimize Core Web Vitals

Google uses Core Web Vitals to measure user experience. Focus on LCP (load speed), INP (interactivity), and CLS (visual stability), especially on product and category pages.

  • ✓Compress Images: Use modern formats like WebP/AVIF and lazy loading.
  • ✓Optimize Code: Minify CSS/JS, remove unused code, and defer non-critical scripts.
  • ✓Fast Hosting & CDN: Reduce server response time (TTFB) with quality hosting and a global CDN. For ultimate performance, consider a headless Shopify setup.

Ensure Flawless Crawling & Indexing

Guide search engines to your most important pages and prevent them from wasting resources on low-value content.

Manage Crawl Budget

Use `robots.txt` to block crawlers from faceted navigations (e.g., `?color=blue`), internal search results, and cart/checkout pages to preserve your crawl budget for valuable URLs.

Fix Duplicate Content

Use `rel="canonical"` tags on product variants and sorted/filtered category pages to point to the primary version, consolidating ranking signals.

Logical Site Architecture

A flat, intuitive site structure ensures any product is findable within 3-4 clicks. Our eCommerce SEO services prioritize creating a scalable architecture.

Handle Out-of-Stock Products

For temporarily out-of-stock items, keep the URL live and offer email notifications. For permanently discontinued products, 301 redirect to a suitable alternative or parent category.

Dynamic XML Sitemaps

Ensure your sitemap automatically updates with new products and correctly reflects `lastmod` dates. This helps search engines discover fresh content faster.

Smart Internal Linking

Link from popular blog posts or pages to relevant product/category pages to pass authority. Explore our SEO blog for more strategies.

Win the Click with Structured Data

Schema markup turns your standard search listing into a rich, informative result that grabs user attention and boosts click-through rates.

Essential eCommerce Schema

Implementing correct schema is vital for product page SEO. Focus on these types:

  • Product Enables price, availability, and star ratings in SERPs.
  • FAQPage Display common questions and answers directly under your listing, perfect for category pages.
  • BreadcrumbList Shows the page's path in search results, improving navigation context.
  • WebSite Enables the sitelinks search box feature for your brand.
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org/",
  "@type": "Product",
  "name": "Stylish Running Sneaker",
  "aggregateRating": {
    "@type": "AggregateRating",
    "ratingValue": "4.9",
    "reviewCount": "128"
  },
  "offers": {
    "@type": "Offer",
    "priceCurrency": "USD",
    "price": "119.99",
    "availability": "https://schema.org/InStock"
  }
}

Advanced & Future-Facing SEO

Stay ahead of the curve by focusing on security, global reach, and the next generation of search.

HTTPS Everywhere

A site-wide SSL certificate (HTTPS) is mandatory for trust and security. Ensure all resources (images, scripts) are loaded over HTTPS to prevent mixed content issues.

International SEO (Hreflang)

If you sell in multiple countries or languages, use `hreflang` tags to tell Google which version of a page to show to users in different regions, preventing duplicate content issues.

AI & Generative Search

The future of search is conversational. Optimizing with AI SEO means focusing on structured data and clear, authoritative content to be featured in AI-powered results.
