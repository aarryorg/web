Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Everything you need to know about our comprehensive Shopify Optimization Plans.

**Your design integrity is guaranteed.** We work exclusively on a **duplicate, unpublished theme** for all speed, CRO, and SEO optimizations. Your live store remains untouched, and we only publish the optimized version after your full review and approval.

We guarantee a minimum Google PageSpeed Score of **90+ on Desktop** and **80+ on Mobile** (for the Pro Plan), alongside verifiable improvements in **Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS)**. This is a commitment; we work until these targets are met.

**AI-Ready SEO** focuses on implementing **Structured Data (Schema Markup)** using **JSON-LD**. This is code that tells advanced search engines and AI models (like Google's SGE or other large language models) exactly what your content means, not just what it says. This significantly improves your eligibility for rich snippets and direct AI answers.

The Starter Plan includes **3 months of support**, while the Pro Plan includes **6 months of post-launch stability monitoring and support**. This ensures your store remains fast and stable long after the optimization is complete.
