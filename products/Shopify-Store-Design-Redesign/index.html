Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How long does a typical Shopify store redesign take?

A standard Shopify redesign project typically takes 4 to 6 weeks from discovery to launch, depending on the complexity and client feedback speed. Full custom builds may take 8+ weeks.

Will redesigning my store affect my current SEO rankings?

We implement a strict 301 redirect and URL mapping plan as part of every project to ensure no loss of SEO value. In fact, our speed and UX improvements often lead to ranking boosts.

What is the difference between a custom theme and a redesign?

A redesign utilizes your existing theme with significant styling and structural updates. A Custom Shopify Theme is built from scratch or based on a blank theme, offering maximum flexibility and minimal code bloat, tailored exactly to your brand's needs.
