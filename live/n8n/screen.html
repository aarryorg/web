Connects your public GitHub page to your local, running n8n instance.

Automation Workflow:

  • **Step 1 (Browser):** Clicking the button requests screen access and displays a local preview.
  • **Step 2 (n8n):** Upon success, the page **sends a Webhook command** to your local n8n.
  • **Step 3 (Automation):** Your n8n workflow takes over to perform the broadcast (e.g., notifying a server, starting a recording process, etc.).
  • **Local Script:** Ensure n8n is running via: ~/Desktop/launch_n8n.sh
