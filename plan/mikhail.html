What is the Dropshipping Business Model? ▼

The Model in 3 Steps:

  • A customer buys a product from our Shopify store at the Retail Price (e.g., $50).
  • We then immediately purchase the product from the supplier at the Wholesale Price (e.g., $20).
  • The supplier ships the product directly to the customer. We never hold inventory.

The Future (Scaling):

This model allows for rapid product testing and expansion (Phase I & II). Once we find consistent winners, we move to private labeling (Phase III) for greater brand control and higher profit margins.

FAQ: What are the key running costs (Management, Supplier, Marketing)? ▼
  • Marketing Spend (Largest Cost): This is the daily investment in ads (Meta/TikTok) that generates the sales. We only scale budgets when Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) is highly profitable.
  • Supplier/COGS: The wholesale cost of the product, automatically deducted when an order is placed.
  • Shopify & App Fees: The monthly subscription fee (Shopify) and fees for essential automation apps (e.g., fulfillment software). Minimal compared to revenue.
  • Store Management Cost: Zero. My expertise is covered by the 50% Net Profit share.
